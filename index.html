About.

A little on where I study and what kind of problems I like.

I'm a Business Analytics Management major at PHINMA University of Iloilo, College of Information Technology Education. Most of my work has come out of capstone projects — built with the same care as production software, because the businesses using them are real.

I like systems where business and technology have to work together: property listings that need to be easy to manage, booking processes that have to stay accurate, and data that needs to turn into useful business insights. That same mindset shows up in how I build — organized booking workflows, reliable databases, analytics dashboards, and tools that help businesses understand their customers, monitor property performance, and make better decisions. For me, analytics isn't just about numbers; it's about turning real business data into something useful.
